The Clifford Hierarchy

Writeups + slides QEC Part 2 · A. Bhople, Q. Hu, T. Middleton, T. Wang, L. Wei, K. Yeon, B. Zhang
Survey WriteupPDF · 6 pp.

Gate teleportation as the motivation for the hierarchy; semi-Clifford and generalized semi-Clifford gates; diagonal gates and their normal form; climbing the hierarchy with CSS codes and Clifford square roots; permutation gates in the third level; logical Clifford synthesis; open problems.

Read the writeup

Presentation SlidesPDF

The group talk. Beyond the writeup, it covers transversal gates, how a code and its qubit partition cap the reachable level, physical versus logical hierarchies, and the ways past that cap (magic states, code switching, lattice surgery, and more).
